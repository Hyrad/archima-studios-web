Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Archima Studios uses an internal application called Archima rclone to provide authorized Archima Studios systems with access to the studio's own Google Drive files.

Google Drive Data

Archima rclone may access, read, create, modify, organize, download, and upload files in Google Drive when authorized by the Google account owner.

This access is used solely for internal Archima Studios file management and synchronization on authorized systems.

Data Storage

Files accessed through Archima rclone may be stored or temporarily cached on authorized Archima Studios systems as required for normal file access and synchronization.

Sharing

Google Drive data accessed by Archima rclone is not sold or used for advertising. It is not shared with third parties except where required to operate the underlying services or by law.

Authorization

Access to Google Drive is granted through Google's OAuth authorization system and can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings.

Google API Services

Archima rclone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to Archima Studios.
